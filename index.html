01 — About

I am a Computer Science undergraduate at Universiti Teknologi PETRONAS, working mainly on data analytics and databases. I like turning messy data into decisions somebody can actually act on, and I like building things that stay simple enough to still make sense a year later.

Outside coursework I lead community projects and run the column writing desk at the UTP Journalism Club — so a fair amount of my time goes into organising people and writing clearly, not only into code.
