01 — About

I am a Computer Science undergraduate at Universiti Teknologi PETRONAS.       How it is built
      
        
      
          Four files: index.html, style.css,
          script.js, assets/. No framework, no build
          step, no package manager, no web font, no analytics and no cookies. A
          Content-Security-Policy of default-src 'none' makes that a
          rule the browser enforces rather than a claim in a readme: the only
          network call the page may make is to api.github.com.
        
      
        
      
          The Projects table is never hardcoded — it is fetched live, cached in
          localStorage for six hours to spare you the wait, and the
          status line always says which of the two you are looking at. The PDF
          export has no PDF library behind it; it decides what is visible and
          hands the page to your browser's own print engine, which is why the
          result keeps selectable text and working links.
